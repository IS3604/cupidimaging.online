Medical Disclaimer

Cupid Imaging LLC provides mobile diagnostic ultrasound imaging services. Ultrasound examinations are diagnostic procedures and are not a substitute for a medical consultation, diagnosis, or treatment by a licensed healthcare provider. Examinations are subject to clinical appropriateness, equipment availability, service location, and applicable professional requirements. Ultrasound findings must be interpreted by an appropriately qualified medical professional. Patients should consult their healthcare provider regarding results and recommendations.

Cupid Imaging LLC does not provide emergency medical services. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department.
