Good to know

Quick answers to common questions.

Call usEmail us
Do you accept insurance?

Not at this time. Cupid Imaging LLC offers self-pay ultrasound examinations only.

Is there a travel fee?

A travel or mileage fee may apply depending on your location. We confirm it before scheduling.

Do you offer emergency reports?

Yes. Emergency reports are delivered the same day with no additional emergency charge. Instant service is available at an additional cost.

Does a request guarantee an appointment?

No. Requests depend on availability, clinical appropriateness and location. Cupid Imaging calls to confirm your appointment.

Can ultrasound replace a doctor visit?

No. Ultrasound is a diagnostic procedure and does not replace evaluation, diagnosis or treatment by a licensed healthcare provider.

Medical Disclaimer

Read moreShow less

Cupid Imaging LLC provides mobile diagnostic ultrasound imaging services. Ultrasound examinations are not a substitute for medical consultation, diagnosis, or treatment by a licensed healthcare provider.

Examinations are subject to clinical appropriateness, equipment availability, service location, and applicable professional requirements. Findings must be interpreted by an appropriately qualified medical professional, and patients should consult their healthcare provider regarding results and recommendations.

Cupid Imaging LLC does not provide emergency medical services. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department. Submitting an appointment request does not guarantee an appointment or confirm a medical diagnosis.
