Good to know

Quick answers about booking, pricing and what to expect from a mobile ultrasound visit.

01Do you accept insurance?

Not at this time. Cupid Imaging LLC currently offers self-pay ultrasound examinations.

02Is there a travel fee?

Additional travel or mileage fees may apply depending on the location. We confirm any applicable travel charge before scheduling.

03How quickly will I receive my report?

Reports are generally available within 12–24 hours, although turnaround times may vary.

04Does an appointment request guarantee an appointment?

No. Appointment requests are subject to availability, clinical appropriateness and location suitability. Your appointment is confirmed separately.

Medical Disclaimer

Cupid Imaging LLC provides mobile diagnostic ultrasound imaging services. Ultrasound examinations are diagnostic procedures and are not a substitute for a medical consultation, diagnosis, or treatment by a licensed healthcare provider. Examinations are subject to clinical appropriateness, equipment availability, service location, and applicable professional requirements. Ultrasound findings must be interpreted by an appropriately qualified medical professional. Patients should consult their healthcare provider regarding results and recommendations.

Cupid Imaging LLC does not provide emergency medical services. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department. We currently accept self-pay and do not bill insurance. Examination prices and applicable travel fees will be confirmed before the appointment. Reports are generally available within 12–24 hours, although turnaround times may vary. Submitting an appointment request does not guarantee an appointment or confirm a medical diagnosis.
